# Urban Heat Mitigation Strategies (Sample)

**Source:** Sample corpus. **Published:** 2024-06-15.

Green infrastructure (trees, parks), cool roofs, reflective pavements, and improved building efficiency reduce local temperatures. Transit and zoning that reduce vehicle miles also lower waste heat.

Implementation depends on climate zone, budget, and equity goals—solutions should be evaluated per neighborhood, not one-size-fits-all.
