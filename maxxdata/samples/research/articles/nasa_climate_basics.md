# Climate Change Basics (Sample — NASA-style summary)

**Source:** Internal training sample (not a verbatim NASA copy). **Published:** 2024-05-01.

Earth’s climate changes naturally, but human activities since the industrial era have increased greenhouse gases. Key points agents should cite from primary agencies when possible:

- Carbon dioxide and other gases trap heat in the atmosphere.
- Observations show warming trends, sea level rise, and ice loss over decades.
- Models project further change depending on future emissions scenarios.

When answering users, prefer linking to NASA, EPA, or peer-reviewed sources rather than this sample alone.
