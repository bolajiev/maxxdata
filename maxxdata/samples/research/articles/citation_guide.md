# Citation Basics for Research Agents

**Source:** Sample training document. **Published:** 2024-01-15.

## Why cite

Citations show where facts come from and let readers verify claims.

## Minimal format

Author or org, title, year, URL or DOI. Example: EPA, "Heat Island Effect", 2024, https://www.epa.gov/heatislands

## When evidence is missing

Respond that the claim cannot be verified from available sources. Do not invent studies.

## Multiple sources

Prefer primary sources (government, peer-reviewed) over opinion blogs for factual claims.
