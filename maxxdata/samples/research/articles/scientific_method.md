# Scientific Method (Sample)

**Source:** Training sample for Maxx research agent. **Published:** 2024-03-01.

Science progresses by testable explanations. A typical flow:

1. **Question** — What do we want to know?
2. **Background research** — What is already established?
3. **Hypothesis** — A falsifiable prediction
4. **Experiment / observation** — Collect evidence
5. **Analysis** — Does evidence support or refute the hypothesis?
6. **Conclusion** — Answer with limits and uncertainty stated

Claims without evidence should be labeled **unknown** or **needs citation**.
