# Peer Review (Sample)

**Source:** Training sample. **Published:** 2024-02-20.

**Peer review** is evaluation of research by experts before publication. It filters obvious flaws but is not a guarantee of truth—retractions and debates still occur.

Types:

- **Single-blind** — reviewers know authors; authors don’t know reviewers
- **Double-blind** — identities hidden both ways
- **Open review** — reports or identities published

For research agents: prefer primary papers and agency reports; use peer-reviewed summaries when available; mark preprints as **not yet peer reviewed**.
